## 1. Purpose

This program defines how THRIVE opens field offices: who earns one, in what order, on what evidence, and with whose money. It converts office expansion from an ad-hoc real-estate decision into a ladder that a leader climbs, with capital released only at defined gates. It exists so that (a) offices open behind proven leaders and proven production, never ahead of them; (b) the company's capital, lease obligations, lead flow, and compliance posture are protected at every step; and (c) every market expansion is repeatable by design — Gate D exists so each office eventually produces its successor.

## 2. Principles

1. **The leader is the scarce asset.** Markets do not create offices; leaders do. We expand where a credible leader exists, not where a map says we should. Market attractiveness is weighted, but leader/team quality dominates the score (60%).
2. **An office is a promotion, not a perk.** Physical space is earned by sustained production and demonstrated leadership, exactly like a title. No one is given an office to see whether they grow into it.
3. **The branch never owns its demand.** All inbound calls and leads route centrally through Lead Tech. The branch receives demand; it does not hold it. If a leader departs, the phones stay behind. This is both a program principle and a mandatory term-sheet item (§7).
4. **Two leaders or none.** Every market campaign recruits at least two credible leader candidates before any launch. No gate advances on a single-person leadership dependency: a second leader is *identified* by Gate A, *developed* by Gate B, and a third exists by Gate C.
5. **Capital and compliance move by gate, not by momentum.** No spend, lease, or expansion occurs between gates because things "feel ready." Each gate requires production evidence, secured lead capacity, and a clean compliance scorecard — all three, every time.

## 4. The Four Levels

### Level 1 — City Candidate
- **Entry:** Market scores above [OWNER SETS: suggested threshold] on the weighted rubric; at least **two** credible leader candidates identified in the leader funnel (§8); central lead capacity for the market confirmed feasible by Lead Tech.
- **Activity:** Remote recruiting and leader courtship. No spend beyond recruiting effort. No space discussions.
- **Exit:** One leader candidate signed to the program with a second candidate still active; first squad activated with ≥ 1.2× lead capacity secured.

### Level 2 — City Incubator
- **Entry:** Level 1 exit met.
- **Standard:** **6 productive agents** (per §3 definition) operating remotely under the local leader, all demand routed centrally, weekly scoreboard live.
- **Exit:** 6 productive agents sustained for [OWNER SETS: suggested 2 consecutive months]; compliance scorecard (§9) green for the market; second leader candidate in "Developing" funnel stage or later.

### Level 3 — Office Qualified
- **Entry:** Level 2 exit met.
- **Standard — all required:**
  1. **12 productive agents**, of whom **≥ 8 are producing consistently** (§3 definitions).
  2. Documented evidence of a **4-day-per-week in-office (or in-person equivalent) working commitment** from the core team — attendance records from the shadow-branch period, not pledges.
  3. **Second leader developing:** named, in role, running at least one squad.
  4. **Local recruiting pipeline:** [OWNER SETS: suggested ≥ N candidates in active licensing] demonstrating the market can replace attrition without central rescue.
  5. Lead capacity ≥ 1.2× the full team's call requirement, secured centrally.
- **Exit:** Shadow Branch protocol (§6) passed, and Gate A criteria met.

### Level 4 — Open Office
- **Entry:** Gate A cleared. Company signs the lease; Launch Balance opens (§7).
- **Standard:** First office is [OWNER SETS: suggested 2,000–2,500 sq ft, 16–20 seats], all-in monthly burn under [OWNER SETS: suggested $8–10K/month].
- **Progression:** Gates B, C, D (§5). An office that falls below Gate A standards for [OWNER SETS: suggested 2 consecutive quarters] triggers a remediation review, not automatic closure — but no further capital releases while below standard.

## 5. The Gates

Capital and expansion authority release only at gates. **Every gate requires, in addition to the criteria below: (a) centrally secured lead capacity ≥ 1.2× the resulting team's call requirement, and (b) a clean compliance scorecard (§9). No exceptions, no partial credit.**

| Gate | Unlocks | Production criteria | Leadership criteria | Lead capacity | Compliance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Open** | Lease signing, Launch Balance, office build-out | 12 producers; 3 months persistency at acceptable level [OWNER SETS: define "acceptable"]; trailing monthly AP ≥ [OWNER SETS: suggested $300K] | Primary leader passed shadow branch; **second leader identified** and in funnel | ≥ 1.2× secured | Clean scorecard |
| **B — Expand** | Additional space/seats, expanded Launch Balance | 25 producers; trailing monthly AP ≥ [OWNER SETS: suggested $625K] | **Second leader developed** (running squads independently) | ≥ 1.2× secured at new scale | Clean scorecard |
| **C — Flagship** | Flagship build-out, market-hub status | 50+ producers; trailing monthly AP ≥ [OWNER SETS: suggested $1.25M] | **Three leaders** operating | ≥ 1.2× secured at new scale | Clean scorecard |
| **D — Replicate** | Authority to seed the next city from this office | Office sustains Gate C standards while exporting | Office has **produced a leader who can leave with 12 producers** and open the next city without degrading the home office below Gate B | Both offices' capacity ≥ 1.2× secured | Clean scorecards in both markets |

## 6. Shadow Branch Protocol

Before any lease is signed, the qualified team operates for **[OWNER SETS: suggested 60–90 days]** as a **shadow branch**: a fully instrumented virtual office with its own scoreboard and its own P&L, as if the lease already existed.

**Shadow scoreboard fields (weekly):**
- Dials, contacts, appointments set/held (from central Lead Tech routing — the branch never sources its own)
- Applications submitted, policies issued, AP written
- Productive-agent count and producing-consistently count (per §3)
- Persistency (trailing, by cohort)
- Recruiting pipeline: candidates by licensing stage
- Attendance: 4-day in-person commitment tracking, by agent
- Compliance items open/closed
- **Shadow P&L:** actual revenue attribution vs. modeled office burn [OWNER SETS: burn model inputs] — would this office have covered its cost this week?

**Meeting cadence:**
- Weekly: leader + program manager, scoreboard review (30 min, numbers only)
- Bi-weekly: leader + Lead Tech, capacity and routing review
- Monthly: owner-level review of shadow P&L and gate trajectory

**Pass:** Shadow P&L at or above breakeven-equivalent for [OWNER SETS: suggested final 60 days], Gate A production criteria met, attendance evidence complete, compliance clean.
**Fail:** Any sustained miss. Failure returns the market to Level 3 (or Level 2 if production collapsed) with a written gap analysis. A failed shadow may re-run once after [OWNER SETS: suggested 90 days]; a second failure returns the market to Level 1 and releases the leader candidates from exclusivity.

## 7. Launch Fund — Terms Outline (for counsel)

This section is a drafting outline for counsel, not executable terms.

- **Structure:** Launch capital of [OWNER SETS: suggested $40–70K per office] held as an **accountable internal Launch Balance** — a company ledger account drawn against approved launch expenses, not a payment to the leader and not a loan to any individual.
- **Ownership:** The **company signs the lease and owns all assets** — furniture, equipment, signage, systems. The leader operates the office; the leader owns none of it.
- **Demand routing (mandatory term-sheet item):** All inbound calls, numbers, and lead sources are provisioned, owned, and routed by central Lead Tech. On any leader departure, all demand routing remains with the company without interruption or transfer. This term is non-negotiable in every launch agreement.
- **Recoupment:** [OWNER SETS: suggested 90-day] grace period from office opening; thereafter the Launch Balance is recouped **from branch economics** (override/branch margin) over [OWNER SETS: suggested 12–18 months]. **Recoupment is never taken by docking individual agent commissions** — counsel to draft this as an explicit prohibition, not an omission.
- **Departure scenarios (counsel to draft each):**
  1. *Leader departs pre-Gate A:* no lease exists; Launch Balance unopened; team and demand remain with company; second leader candidate assumes the funnel.
  2. *Leader departs post-opening, balance outstanding:* company retains office, assets, team, and all demand routing; recoupment of remaining balance treated per [OWNER SETS: write-off vs. successor-branch assumption]; second leader elevation is the default continuity plan.
  3. *Leader departs at Gate D (sanctioned replication):* the sanctioned path — departure *with* 12 producers to open the next city under a new launch agreement; home-office continuity guaranteed by the remaining leaders.
  4. *Office closure:* company disposes of lease/assets; treatment of unrecouped balance [OWNER SETS].
- **Compliance representations:** launch agreement includes leader's ongoing obligation to maintain the §9 scorecard and consent to regulatory-history screening.

## 8. Leader Funnel and Per-City Dashboard

**Leader funnel stages:** Identified → Contacted → Courting → Committed (signed to program) → Incubating (running Level 2 squad) → Developing (second-leader track) → Qualified (passed shadow) → Operating (post-Gate A) → Replicating (Gate D track). *Program rule: no market campaign proceeds past "Courting" until ≥ 2 candidates are at that stage or beyond.*

**Per-city CRM dashboard fields:**
- Market: name, wave assignment, weighted score (60/20/10/10 breakdown), level (1–4), current gate target
- Leaders: candidates by funnel stage; second-leader status; regulatory-screen status/date
- Production: productive agents, producing-consistently count, trailing monthly AP, persistency
- Leads: secured central capacity vs. required (the 1.2× ratio, displayed as a number)
- Recruiting: local pipeline by licensing stage
- Compliance: scorecard status (green/red per §9 item), open complaints
- Capital: Launch Balance drawn/remaining, recoupment schedule status
- Shadow branch: start date, weeks elapsed, shadow P&L trend, pass/fail projection

**Compliance scorecard (all green required to advance any gate; no office opens without all four):**
1. State call-recording consent rules implemented in the recording/dialer configuration for that state.
2. All agents licensed and appointed for every product sold.
3. Complaint handling routed to the central register with the branch having no ability to intercept.
4. Leader (and second leader, from Gate B) passed regulatory-history screen.
