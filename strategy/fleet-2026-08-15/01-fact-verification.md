## Corrections the advisory should absorb

1. **Phoenix**: cite one brokerage per market. The CBRE-consistent pair is
   19.1% / $32.30; the 23.3% belongs to a different tracker's broader
   inventory. Directionally both say "tenant-favorable" — the error is
   sourcing hygiene, not conclusion.
2. **Consolidation framing**: the FE-specific AmeriLife acquisitions are
   from **2020–2021**, not recent. The 2025 deal (Crump) is brokerage/wealth
   distribution. If the advisory implies current FE consolidation momentum,
   that overstates it; the demonstrated FE buyer appetite is real but its
   freshest cited evidence is four to five years old.
3. **Charlotte labor-pool numbers** are the one load-bearing unverified
   input — verify at BLS before using them to rank markets.
