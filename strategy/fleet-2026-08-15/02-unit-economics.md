Delivered 2026-08-15. Provenance discipline: **[published]** = appears in the
cited source; **[inference]** = calculation from published figures;
**[commentary]** = practitioner/vendor claims (lead vendors and recruiting

## The finding that reframes the plan

**$25M/month = $300M/year of FE annualized premium ≈ 29% of the entire
2024 LIMRA-measured FE market ($1.05B AP, 1.06M policies).** The scale
assumption breaks before the productivity assumption does: the plan's
endpoint requires either taking a dominant share of the measured FE market
or expanding the market itself — which materially strengthens the case
(already made in Part 2) that the vertical expansion beyond FE is a
prerequisite, not an option.

SelectQuote is the SEC-documented cautionary case [published, High]: FE lapse
under-accrual corrections of $2.0M (FY20), $6.1M (FY21), $2.4M (Q1 FY22),
plus a $145M cohort writedown; FE premium growth swung +82% YoY to −26% YoY
inside a year as they pulled back. Growth booked on advances and LTV
assumptions, clawed back by persistency reality.

## Implication table (avg $950/policy, 22% inbound close, 21.5 selling days)

| Blended AP/agent/mo | Active agents for $25M | Inbound calls/month needed | Calls/agent/day |
|---|---|---|---|
| $12K | 2,083 | ~120,000 | ~2.7 |

| $25K | 1,000 | ~120,000 | ~5.6 |

Lead-budget cross-check [inference]: 120K inbound calls at $50–$85/call =
**$6.0M–$10.2M/month of lead spend** — 24–41% of the $25M AP, before agent
comp.

## Margin mechanics [inference, med confidence]

At 120–135% FYC contracts, a 13% net margin on AP balances only around:
agent comp 60–80 pts, lead cost 25–35 pts, opex 10–15 pts, chargeback/lapse
provision 10–20 pts of FYC — and only while 13-month persistency holds
≥~75%. Every 5 points of persistency lost costs roughly 5–7 points of FYC in
chargebacks on advanced business — enough to erase most of a 13% margin.

## Bottom line (as delivered)

1. $25K AP/agent/month is a **best-case, not planning-safe**, blended assumption.
2. Plan at **$12K–$15K blended** ($15–20K if premium inbound TV quality is proven) — roughly **doubling** the agent headcount and recruiting funnel.
3. The binding constraint is **lead supply**: ~120K quality inbound calls/month at $6–10M cost, against a total measured market the plan would need ~29% of.
4. Model **65–75% telesales persistency**, not 80%+, and reserve 10–20 pts of FYC for chargebacks — SelectQuote is the documented failure mode.
5. The 13% margin survives only if persistency ≥~75% and CPA ≤~$350; stress-test at 65% / $425 before committing headcount.
