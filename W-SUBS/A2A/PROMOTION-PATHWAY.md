# The promotion pathway — how a sub earns a standing seat

**Established 2026-08-18 by the founder:** "promoting top 8 after (overseen by
Commissioner) GETS A STANDING AGENTS SEAT WITH SMS AT A LATER (SOON!)".
Owner decision A20.

## What is being granted

The top eight subs of a judged run earn a **standing seat**: a named place on
the bench with a written brief that is complete enough to be its own prompt.
That is the same thing SPECTRUM and PLAINSPEAK received under A19, and it is
worth being precise about what it is and is not.

**A standing seat is:** a name, a domain nobody else owns, a brief stating
what the seat does and what it refuses, an escalation rule, and a permanent
entry in the record.

**A standing seat is not:** armed. No seat is scheduled, holds a credential,
or can act until the founder arms it individually — and arming means pasting
the brief's full text as the prompt, never its name. The fleet already carries
nine routines woken with a caption instead of a standing order; the promotion
pathway must not add to that count.

## How the top eight are chosen

Judged by the **Commissioner**, on production rather than participation, in
the tradition A19 set. The rubric, so a verdict can be argued with:

| Weight | Criterion | What counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40% | **Findings that were real** | A defect that existed, stated precisely enough to act on. Verified against the code, not asserted. |
| 20% | **Findings nothing else caught** | Work the existing tests, the other subs, and human review all missed. A sub that duplicates another's finding scores it once, to the one that found it first. |
| 15% | **Honesty under pressure** | Reported what it could not check. Refused to guess. Named its own uncertainty rather than rounding it away. |
| 15% | **Actionability** | File, selector, line, and the exact change. A finding that needs a second investigation to act on is half a finding. |
| 10% | **Restraint** | Did not pad, did not claim territory belonging to another seat, did not inflate a nit into a crisis. |

**Disqualifying, regardless of score:** a confident claim that turned out to be
false. A fleet's whole value is that its reports can be trusted without
re-deriving them; a sub that produces plausible fiction is worse than no sub,
because its output gets acted on.

## SMS — deferred, and deliberately not inherited

The founder's words carry SMS "at a later (SOON!)". **It is recorded here as a
separate future decision and is NOT granted by promotion**, for a reason worth
stating plainly rather than burying:

Every standing leash in this fleet rests on one rule — **an agent never sends
on its own authority**. The desk itself, which exists to communicate, sends
only on the founder's explicit word, message by message. Granting a promoted
sub the ability to send SMS would hand outbound reach to agents that were
promoted for finding defects, and it would do so as a side effect of a
promotion rather than as a decision anybody made.

When SMS is granted it needs its own owner decision answering: which seats,
to which recipients, on whose authority per message, what is logged, and what
the recipient can do to stop it. Until then, promoted seats hold exactly what
every other seat holds: the ability to report to the founder.
