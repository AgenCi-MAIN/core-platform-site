About this collection

These ten portraits are the output of a structured creative relay described in the Ten Agents, Ten Portraits field plan. Each portrait is produced by a different agent in the chain, under the direction of a human creative director.

The placeholder illustrations above represent the compositional and chromatic framework each portrait will follow. Final artwork is generated from a versioned prompt pack, inspected by quality assurance, and archived with full provenance.

AI-made disclosure: Every portrait in this collection is AI-generated artwork. The creative direction, approval, and curation are human-led. No portrait represents a real person. The work is described accurately as "AI-made" in accordance with the project's language rules. Prompt provenance and generation settings are archived for each image.
