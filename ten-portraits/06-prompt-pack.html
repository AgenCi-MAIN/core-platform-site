1. Master Prompt Template

The base prompt that establishes every invariant: style, composition, format, lighting, and quality. Bracketed tokens are the only elements that change between portraits. Everything outside the brackets is locked.

Portrait of a [GENDER_PRESENTATION] strategic operative, head and shoulders, facing [GAZE_DIRECTION], with [HAIR_DESCRIPTION] and [DISTINGUISHING_FEATURE]. Wearing [OUTFIT]. [ROLE_SIGNIFIER]. Expression: [EXPRESSION]. Background: a dark, abstracted environment of deep indigo-black (#16142b) with subtle [BACKGROUND_ELEMENT] in [ACCENT_COLOR_NAME] ([ACCENT_HEX]) glowing softly behind the figure. Lighting: cinematic three-quarter key light from the [LIGHT_DIRECTION], warm fill from below, cool rim light separating the figure from the background. Subtle [ACCENT_COLOR_NAME] light spill on the near cheekbone. Style: digital illustration with painterly rendering, visible brushwork in shadows, sharp detail on eyes and expression. Matte finish, no specular highlights on skin. Color palette restricted to ink (#16142b), paper (#fffaf2), [ACCENT_COLOR_NAME] ([ACCENT_HEX]), and one muted secondary from {violet, coral, gold, mint, blue}. Cohesive world: intelligence analysts in a fictional setting that merges global finance with AI systems. Format: square, 1024x1024. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI elements. No real celebrity or public-figure likenesses. Content safe for professional use.

2. Variant System

Each portrait varies across nine controllable dimensions. Every other aspect of the prompt is locked to enforce visual cohesion across the collection.

Variable tokens

Token Controls Constraint
GENDER_PRESENTATION Perceived gender of the figure Balanced across the set: 5 male-presenting, 4 female-presenting, 1 androgynous
GAZE_DIRECTION Where the eyes look Mix of direct-to-viewer and three-quarter; no two adjacent portraits share direction
HAIR_DESCRIPTION Hair style, length, color Diverse across the set; no two identical
DISTINGUISHING_FEATURE One memorable facial or accessory detail Subtle, professional; no fantasy elements
OUTFIT Clothing visible in frame Variations on tactical-professional attire; all share the same world
ROLE_SIGNIFIER One environmental or prop detail that connects to the role Integrated naturally, never held up to camera
EXPRESSION Emotional tone of the face Range from focused to warm; no extremes
BACKGROUND_ELEMENT Abstract motif behind the figure Geometric, data-inspired, or atmospheric; always soft-focus
ACCENT_COLOR Primary accent from the palette Each of the five chromatic colors used exactly twice across the ten portraits
LIGHT_DIRECTION Key light placement Alternates left/right to prevent monotony

Locked invariants

  • Square 1:1 composition, head-and-shoulders framing
  • Digital illustration style with painterly rendering
  • Dark indigo-black (#16142b) background base
  • Cinematic three-quarter lighting model
  • Matte skin finish, sharp eye detail
  • No text, logos, watermarks, UI overlays
  • No real-person likenesses
  • Professional, workplace-safe content
  • Shared fictional world (finance + AI intelligence)

4. Universal Negative Prompt

Apply this negative prompt to every generation. It covers quality exclusions, content safety, and stylistic consistency. For Midjourney, append with --no; for Stable Diffusion, use the negative prompt field; for DALL-E, incorporate exclusions into the main prompt text (DALL-E does not support a separate negative field).

Consistency strategy across the set

  • Generate Portrait 01 (Mission Keeper) first and iterate until it anchors the style
  • In Midjourney, use the accepted Portrait 01 as --sref for all subsequent portraits
  • In Stable Diffusion, lock the checkpoint model and LoRA stack after Portrait 01 is accepted
  • Maintain the same post-processing pipeline (if any) across all ten
  • Review all ten as a grid at 256px thumbnails before finalizing — inconsistencies are visible at small scale that disappear when viewing one at a time
  • If one portrait drifts from the set, regenerate it rather than post-processing it to match
