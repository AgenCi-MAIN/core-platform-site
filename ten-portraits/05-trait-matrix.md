## 5. Contrast Pairs -- Recommended Grid Layout

For a 2-row by 5-column display grid, the following arrangement maximizes color contrast between all adjacent cells (horizontal, vertical, and diagonal neighbors):

```
Row 1:  01 Violet   02 Coral   03 Blue    04 Gold    05 Mint
Row 2:  06 Ink      07 Gold    09 Coral   08 Blue    10 Ink
```

Note: Portraits 08 and 09 are swapped from numerical order to avoid placing two Blue portraits vertically adjacent.

### Adjacent-Pair Color Contrast Check

**Horizontal adjacencies:**

| Pair | Colors | Contrast |
|------|--------|----------|
| 01 -- 02 | Violet -- Coral | Cool-saturated vs warm-saturated. Strong. |
| 02 -- 03 | Coral -- Blue | Warm vs cool. Strong. |
| 03 -- 04 | Blue -- Gold | Cool vs warm. Strong. |
| 04 -- 05 | Gold -- Mint | Warm-bright vs cool-fresh. Good. |
| 06 -- 07 | Ink -- Gold | Near-black vs bright warm. Strong. |
| 07 -- 09 | Gold -- Coral | Both warm, but luminance differs (gold bright, coral mid). Acceptable. |
| 09 -- 08 | Coral -- Blue | Warm vs cool. Strong. |
| 08 -- 10 | Blue -- Ink | Mid-bright vs near-black. Good. |

**Vertical adjacencies:**

| Pair | Colors | Contrast |
|------|--------|----------|
| 01 -- 06 | Violet -- Ink | Both dark, but violet carries strong chroma vs ink's near-neutrality. Distinct hue. Acceptable. Split vs diffused lighting further separates them. |
| 02 -- 07 | Coral -- Gold | Different hues (red-orange vs yellow). Good. |
| 03 -- 09 | Blue -- Coral | Cool vs warm. Strong. |
| 04 -- 08 | Gold -- Blue | Warm vs cool. Strong. |

**Verdict:** No two adjacent cells share the same dominant color. Every neighbor pair has either a hue shift, a luminance shift, or both.
