On the third day

Anyone could have become the owner.

Identity was arriving as an ordinary request header, and the application believed it. Safe only while a hosting platform stood in front and stripped those headers away. The moment THRIVE self-hosted — which independence requires — that protection was gone. An email address was all it would have taken.

Found, and closed

Held shut by machinery,
not by memory.

Identitysigned session · only this app can mint it
Retired headersignored entirely
Two test suitesfail the build if it ever reopens

The tests send precisely the request that would have worked. Memory is unreliable. Machinery is less so.

The spine

Two checks.
Every single request.

First — identitywho you are
Second — membershipan active record, carrying a role
The defaultdeny

Identity alone grants nothing whatsoever. Anyone on earth can pass the first check — that is rather the point. The second is what protects the company. If the database cannot be reached, the portal refuses everyone, including its owners.

The record

Every decision is
written down.

portal.accessallow · active member
audit.viewdeny · capability not held
portal.accessdeny · not a member

Permitted and refused alike, into a log never edited and never deleted. Who opened a client's book. Who played a recording. Who granted whom access, and when. That record is the reason agents can safely be added at all.

What comes next

Colleagues. Each one narrow.

Call reviewscores every call · flags coaching
Script keepertraces the language that actually closed
Pipelinewatches the book for stalls
Complianceconsent · retention · disclosure

Each enters exactly as a person does — a record, a role, and precisely the capabilities that role holds. Not one more. An agent with one job can be judged. An agent asked to do everything cannot.

The permanent denials

Two things no agent
will ever hold.

members.managenever
audit.viewnever

An agent that can grant itself access has no ceiling. An agent that can read the record of its own refusals can learn to stop leaving one. These are design rules, not defaults awaiting reconsideration. And revoking any of us is a single row.
